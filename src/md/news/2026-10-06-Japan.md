Prof. Anna Matuszyńska has been invited to the Japan–Germany Bilateral Meeting 2026, entitled "New Strategies in Cyanobacterial Research: From Decoding Cellular Functions to Pioneering Innovations." The meeting will take place from October 29 to November 3, 2026, at Hiroshima University in Higashi-Hiroshima, Japan, bringing together researchers from Japan and Germany working on emerging directions in cyanobacterial research.

Prof. Matuszyńska will give an invited talk titled "Integrative Hybrid Modelling of Photosynthesis to Quantify the Biotechnological Potential of Cyanobacteria," presenting CPBL's work on combining mechanistic and data-driven approaches to understand and quantify photosynthetic processes in cyanobacteria.

She will also present a poster titled "Living models for collaborative science: introducing mxlweb for easier and faster model development," highlighting tools developed to make mathematical modelling more accessible and facilitate collaborative model development.

We are very excited about this opportunity to join the Japan–Germany meeting, exchange ideas with researchers working on cyanobacteria, and strengthen CPBL's collaborations with Japanese research groups.
