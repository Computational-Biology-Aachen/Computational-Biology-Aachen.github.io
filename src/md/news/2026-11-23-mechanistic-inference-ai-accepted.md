We are delighted to announce that our perspective article "When Everyone Can Build a Model: Mechanistic Inference in the Age of AI" was accepted for publication in Frontiers in Systems Biology on 9 November 2026. It will appear in the section Multiscale Mechanistic Modeling, as part of the Research Topic "Grey-Box Modeling in Biomedicine: Integrating Mechanistic Knowledge and AI Across Scales".

The article is a collaboration between our group and Prof. Adèle H. Ribeiro (Causal and Explainable Artificial Intelligence in Life Sciences), both part of the Center for Computational Life Sciences at RWTH Aachen University.

AI tools have made building grey-box models of biological systems easy, but interpreting them is as hard as ever: a model that predicts well does not prove that its inferred mechanism is the real one, and several different models often fit the same data equally well. We argue that causal inference and ensemble analyses together show what the data support, what remains open, and which experiment would settle the question. You can read more about the ideas behind the article in our [preprint announcement](/news/2026-11-11-mechanistic-inference-ai).
