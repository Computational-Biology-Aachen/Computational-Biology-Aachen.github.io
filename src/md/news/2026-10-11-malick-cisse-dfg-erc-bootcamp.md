We are pleased to share that Dr. El Hadji Malick Cisse, a Port to Europe fellow postdoctoral researcher in the Computational Biology group at RWTH Aachen University, was selected to participate in the competitive RWTH DFG–ERC Grant Writing Bootcamp, held from September 28 to October 2, 2026, at Kloster Steinfeld, Germany.

![Malick and fellow participants during the DFG–ERC Bootcamp](/news/DFG_ERC_Bootcamp_2026_1.jpg)

The intensive five-day programme provided practical training in developing competitive research proposals for major funding opportunities, including those offered by the German Research Foundation (DFG) and the European Research Council (ERC).

During the bootcamp, Malick further developed his research proposal on integrating transcriptional dynamics with photosynthetic regulation through neural ordinary differential equations to identify physiological tipping points under multifactorial environmental stress.

![Participants at Kloster Steinfeld during the DFG–ERC Bootcamp](/news/DFG_ERC_Bootcamp_2026_2.jpg)

The programme also offered valuable opportunities for interdisciplinary discussions, constructive feedback, and networking with researchers from different scientific fields.

This experience represents an important step toward Malick's goal of establishing an independent research programme at the interface of experimental plant physiology and computational biology.
