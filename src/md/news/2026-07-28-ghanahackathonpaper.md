In March of 2026, the Photosynthesis Hackathon was held in Ghana, bringing together researchers from different fields of study in Europe and Africa to deepen understanding of photosynthesis. Most European researchers measure photosynthesis using complex parameter sets, but on a lab scale. Most African researchers directly measure photosynthesis in the field. This creates a disparity in knowledge and approaches between researchers who are actually studying the same phenomenon. Therefore, with the help of the hackathon, discussions and collaborations were fostered to bridge this gap. To showcase not only the effectiveness of an international hackathon, but also the scientific results that may come out of it, a publication has been written.

This publication includes information on the hackathon and its outcomes, delving deeper into the photosynthetic parameters measured and computational use cases to further the understanding of photosynthesis. The preprint of this publication can be found at [https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.24.740625](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.24.740625).
