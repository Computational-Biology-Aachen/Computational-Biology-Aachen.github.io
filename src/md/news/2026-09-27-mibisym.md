The [MibiNet International Symposium](https://www.sfb1535.hhu.de/international-symposium/), held at the **Kardinal Schulte Haus in Bergisch Gladbach**, brings together world-leading experts and talented early-career researchers to discuss current developments in microbial community research. Prof. Anna Matuszyńska will represent CPBL with an invited talk titled **"Metabolic games and race for carbon in a heterotroph consortium."**, presenting the recent collaborative work of our lab and the group of Prof. Stefan Schuster in Jena, recently published in *Open Biology*
*.

In her talk, Prof. Matuszyńska will present an eco-evolutionary mathematical model of a heterotrophic tripartite community comprising public metabolizers that produce a shared resource, private metabolizers that retain the ability to exploit resources intracellularly, and cheaters that consume the public resource without contributing to its production.

We wish Prof. Matuszyńska an inspiring symposium and look forward to the discussions in Bergisch Gladbach!
